# Supermart Grocery Sales — Executive Performance Dashboard

## Project Overview
This project was completed as part of the **Sapphire IQ Data Analytics Internship**. The primary objective is to design an interactive executive-level dashboard in Microsoft Excel for management (Ram & Supermart Leadership) to analyze sales trends, category profitability, discount impact, and geographic performance across Tamil Nadu without reviewing raw dataset records.

---

## Key Performance Indicators (2015 – 2018)

* **Total Sales Revenue:** ₹14,956,982.00 (₹1.50 Cr)
* **Total Profit:** ₹3,747,121.20 (₹37.47 Lakhs)
* **Overall Profit Margin:** 25.05%
* **Total Orders:** 9,994
* **Average Discount:** 22.68%

---

## Key Findings & Business Insights

### 1. Overall Sales Performance (Objective 1)
* **Revenue Drivers:** Total sales reached **₹14.96M** with a steady year-over-year growth trajectory from **₹2.98M** in 2015 to **₹4.98M** in 2018.
* **Category Contribution:** Sales are evenly distributed across 7 major grocery categories. **Eggs, Meat & Fish** leads total sales at **₹2.27M** (15.16%), closely followed by **Snacks** (**₹2.24M**) and **Food Grains** (**₹2.12M**).
* **Regional Contribution:** The **West** region generated the highest sales (**₹4.80M** / 32.1%) and profit (**₹1.19M**), followed by the **East** region (**₹4.25M**).

### 2. Category & Product Performance (Objective 2)
* **Top Sub-Categories:** *Health Drinks* (Beverages), *Soft Drinks* (Beverages), and *Cookies* (Bakery) generated the highest sales volumes.
* **High Sales / Lower Margin Areas:** While all categories maintain a ~24%–25% profit margin, **Oil & Masala** recorded the lowest average profit margin (**24.43%**, ₹497.9K profit) despite generating **₹2.04M** in sales. Specific sub-categories like *Spices* (23.82%) and *Masalas* (24.23%) have higher relative cost ratios.

### 3. Profitability & Discount Analysis (Objective 3)
* **Discount Impact:** The average discount offered across 9,994 orders was **22.68%**. Correlation analysis shows discounts are evenly distributed across categories without eroding base profit margins (which remain stable around ~25%).
* **Most Profitable Categories:** **Snacks** (**₹568.18K** profit) and **Eggs, Meat & Fish** (**₹567.36K** profit) delivered the largest total profit dollars.

### 4. Geographic Performance (Objective 4)
* **Top Performing Cities:** 
  1. **Kanyakumari:** ₹706,764 Sales | ₹172,217 Profit
  2. **Vellore:** ₹676,550 Sales | ₹174,073 Profit
  3. **Bodi:** ₹667,177 Sales | ₹173,655 Profit
  4. **Tirunelveli:** ₹659,812 Sales | ₹165,169 Profit
  5. **Perambalur:** ₹659,738 Sales | ₹171,132 Profit
* **Performance Variance:** **Tenkasi** (24.27% margin) and **Namakkal** (24.31% margin) showed slightly lower profit margins relative to their sales volume compared to top performers like **Karur** (26.36% margin).

---

## Actionable Recommendations
1. **Supply Chain Optimization for Oil & Masala:** Renegotiate supplier pricing for *Spices* and *Masalas* to bring their profit margins up to the 25.4% benchmark set by Snacks.
2. **Targeted Expansion in West & East Regions:** Allocate 60% of regional promotional budget to West and East clusters where consumer demand and profitability are highest.
3. **Discount Standardization:** Maintain discount caps around 20%–22% across high-demand grocery staples during festive periods to prevent unnecessary revenue leakage.
